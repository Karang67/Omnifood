Privacy Policy

Data privacy rules, cookie parameters, and security policies.

Omnifood values your privacy. This statement explains our procedures regarding personal data collection, usage, and protection when ordering meal subscriptions.

1. Personal Data Collected

To provide subscription food deliveries, we collect profile registration details: name, email address, mobile number, delivery residential/office address, and location details.

2. Sourcing & Processing

We use your address coordinates exclusively to calculate optimal routing times, assign delivery partners, and coordinate food logistics. We use industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS) to process payments safely.

3. Cookie Policy

Our server cookies track account status (active tokens) and user choices (delivery locations) to streamline account configurations. We never sell your personal data profiles to third-party databases.
