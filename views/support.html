Frequently Asked Questions

How does the monthly subscription plan work?

Subscriptions deliver 1 chef-crafted organic meal daily (or 10 meals/month depending on Pro plans) to your designated address. You can adjust your daily delivery time slots dynamically from the user menu.

Can I pause or edit my subscription?

Yes, absolutely! You can pause billing, cancel the plan, or adjust your ingredients preferences directly from your account page, with zero penalty rates.

What areas do you currently cover?

We currently serve customers in Lisbon, San Francisco, London, Berlin, and are actively scaling operations across hubs in Delhi NCR.
